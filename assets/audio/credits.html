Adapted for the playground

We trimmed, filtered, levelled and looped the recordings, stabilized the pitch of selected engine sections, and softened the tyre recording’s upper frequencies. Playback follows simulated RPM, engine load and tyre slip.

The fictional cars share representative sound families. These are not exact recordings of each car’s engine or calibrated acoustic models. The short source clips need pitch shifting at higher revs. The electric sedan retains its quiet motor sound and is silent at rest.

Audio is bundled locally and starts only when you enable Sound. Full attribution and adaptation notes.
